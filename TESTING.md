# NeuroPass V1 testing checklist

## Guarantee engine
- 60 days at 2.5 hrs/day returns 260 guaranteed minimum.
- 60 days at 5 hrs/day returns approximately 310 to 315 due to spaced repetition limits.
- 90 days at 3.5 hrs/day returns at least 330.
- 180 days at 1.5 hrs/day returns approximately 260 to 280.
- 180 days at 5 hrs/day returns 395, never 400 guaranteed.

## Compliance
- 80%+ rolling average and no low weeks gives Fully active.
- One consecutive week below 70% remains active with catch-up action.
- Two consecutive weeks below 70% gives At risk and a human touchpoint.
- Three consecutive weeks below 70% gives Paused, not voided.
- Final 14 days always gives Crisis Mode protected, never paused.

## Retention
- Initial wrong answer schedules re-serve after 3 days.
- Another wrong answer schedules after 2 days.
- Three consecutive correct answers marks the mistake mastered.
- Topic reviews schedule on days 3, 7, 14 and 30, then monthly to exam date.

## UX
- No skip reason is requested.
- Low Energy Mode reduces the next session to a lite path without removing compliance credit.
- Accommodation toggles are all independently editable.
- Student navigation contains no Admin controls.
- CFA is waitlist only.
- Offline banner appears when disconnected and queued progress syncs after reconnection.
